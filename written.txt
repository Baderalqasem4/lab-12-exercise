Q3. What happens to the bounding boxes with update + fullRefit?

    When the circles move, the bounding boxes can become larger and overlap more because
    the circles remain in their original BVH nodes. The tree structure does not change, which makes
    intersection queries less efficient over time.

Q4. What happens with reset (rebuild every frame) instead?

    Rebuilding the BVH every frame groups the circles based on their current positions. 
    This keeps the bounding boxes tighter and reduces overlap, which makes intersection queries
    more efficient. But rebuilding the tree is more expensive than refitting it.

Q5. Why are some circles drawn with their sides cut off?

    Some circles are drawn with their sides cut off because their radius is not considered when choosing
    their position. Since only pixels inside the screen are rendered, the parts outside the screen are cut off.
    Overlapping circles can also hide parts of each other because the last circle hit determines the pixel color.
